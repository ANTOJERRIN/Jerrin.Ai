# Chips Reel Starter Kit — Script → Edit → Enhance

A practical "do this every time" workflow, written from the customer's eye view (what makes someone stop scrolling and crave the chips), pulled from your Chips Reel Workflow Prompt and cut down to what actually gets a reel made.

---

## PART 1 — Script Writing (Start Here, Before You Shoot or Edit)

Every reel script has 4 blocks. Write these as plain text before touching any footage.

### The 4-Block Script Template
```
[HOOK - 0:00-0:02]
What stops the scroll? (visual + line)

[BUILD - 0:02-0:07]
Why should they keep watching? (craving/curiosity/relatability)

[PAYOFF - 0:07-0:12]
The crunch, the reveal, the offer, the taste moment

[CTA - 0:12-0:15]
What do you want them to do? (order/comment/tag/visit)
```

### Hook Formulas (customer-first, not brand-first)
Write 3 hooks per reel, pick the strongest:
- **POV hook:** "POV: it's 6pm and you haven't had your chips fix yet"
- **Problem hook:** "Nobody tells you this about store-bought chips..."
- **Curiosity hook:** "This is why our chips sound different when you bite them"
- **Number hook:** "3 flavors, 1 winner — you decide"
- **Reveal hook:** Start mid-action (oil sizzling, packet ripping, chip mid-air) — no intro line needed, just visual shock

Rule: the hook must work with sound OFF. Instagram autoplay is muted for most viewers — the visual has to carry it.

### Reel-Type Script Skeletons (from your content strategy list)

| Reel Type | Script Shape |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Close-up texture shot → crunch sound → ingredient callout text → pack shot |
| **Offer** | Price reveal animation → urgency line ("today only") → CTA |
| **Festival** | Festive visual opener → brand tie-in line → gifting angle → CTA |
| **Customer** | Real customer reaction (unscripted feel) → text overlay quote → logo end card |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | Raw process shot (frying/packing) → 1-line caption → "this is how it's made" |
| **Educational** | Question hook ("Why do chips go stale?") → quick answer → tie to your product |
| **Trend-based** | Trending audio/format first, chips content layered in — don't force the fit |

### Writing Rule
Keep spoken/text words under **20 words total** for a 15-second reel. If your script reads like a paragraph, it's too long — cut it to phrases.

## PART 2 — Video Editor Workflow (Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve / CapCut)

Once the script is locked, follow this order every time. Don't skip steps — this is what separates "amateur clip" from "brand reel."

### Step-by-Step Cutting Order
1. **Import & organize** — one project folder per reel: `Raw / Selects / Audio / Exports`
2. **Vertical sequence** — 1080×1920, 30fps (or 60fps if you shot slow-mo)
3. **Rough cut** — drop only the clips matching your 4-block script, in order, no effects yet
4. **Beat sync** — add music first, mark beat points, snap your cuts to the beats (Premiere: Essential Sound → tap markers; Resolve: Fairlight markers)
5. **Fine cut** — trim every clip to its tightest version; kill dead air ruthlessly
6. **Speed ramp** — slow motion on the crunch/pour/reveal moment, snap back to normal speed right after (Premiere: Time Remapping; Resolve: Retime Curve in Edit page)
7. **Motion + zoom** — subtle keyframed scale (100%→108%) on key shots for energy; avoid over-zooming on food texture, it looks fake
8. **Transitions** — whip transition between location/scene changes, flash transition (white frame, 2-3 frames) on the reveal moment, match cut between similar shapes (chip shape to packet shape, etc.)
9. **Color grade** — do this AFTER cuts are locked, not before (see grading looks below)
10. **Text/motion graphics** — add captions, price/CTA animation, logo reveal last
11. **Audio mix** — music bed -14 LUFS approx, crunch SFX peaks slightly above dialogue level, no clipping
12. **Export** — see settings below

### Quick Color Grade Recipes
- **Warm food look:** +5 warmth, lift shadows slightly, boost orange/yellow saturation (this makes fried food look appetizing)
- **Bright Instagram look:** raise exposure +0.3, lower contrast slightly, punch up saturation +10
- **Premium commercial look:** desaturate background, isolate/boost product color with a power window/qualifier
- **Cinematic look:** teal shadows, warm highlights, slight vignette

### Export Settings (Instagram/Reels/Shorts)
- 1080×1920, H.264, 30fps, ~10 Mbps, AAC audio, under 90 seconds
- Cover frame: pick your strongest still, not frame 1

## PART 3 — Enhancing With AI Tools (What to Actually Use, and For What)

Don't use all of these on every reel — pick 1-2 per reel based on the gap you need filled.

| Tool | Use It For | Skip It For |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT/Gemini** | Drafting hook variations, caption copy, hashtag sets, content calendar | Final creative decisions — your eye on the actual footage beats generic output |
| **Adobe Firefly** | Generating background plates, extending a shot's edges (Generative Fill) for reframing to vertical | Making the actual chips look real — AI food textures still look synthetic |
| **Premiere AI (Text-based editing, Enhance Speech)** | Cleaning up voiceover audio, auto-captioning customer testimonial reels | Replacing your beat-sync editing judgment |
| **DaVinci AI (Magic Mask, Speed Warp)** | Isolating the product for color grading, smoothing slow-motion crunch shots | Full auto-cut — still trim manually for pacing |
| **Runway** | B-roll you can't shoot (abstract flavor/spice motion, stylized transitions) | Primary product shots |
| **CapCut AI (auto-captions, auto-reframe)** | Fast turnaround reels, repurposing horizontal footage to vertical | Your hero/flagship product reels — do those manually for quality |
| **ElevenLabs** | Clean AI voiceover for educational/BTS reels if you don't want to record VO | Anything customer-testimonial (needs to sound real) |
| **Suno/Udio** | Custom jingle/sting for your logo reveal or brand sound | Main background music — licensed/trending audio still performs better on Reels |

### The One Rule for AI Use
AI speeds up the boring parts (captions, voiceover cleanup, hashtag lists, background extension). It should never touch the parts that make people crave the chips — the actual crunch shot, the real texture, the real customer reaction. Customers can tell the difference, and food reels live or die on believability.

---

## Your Repeatable Loop
1. Write the 4-block script + 3 hook options
2. Shoot/select footage matching the script
3. Cut in the 12-step editor order above
4. Run only the AI tools that fill a real gap
5. Export, pick a strong cover frame, post

Keep this file as your checklist — copy the 4-block template into a new note every time you plan a reel.
